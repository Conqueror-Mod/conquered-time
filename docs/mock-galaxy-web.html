Level 0 — Galaxies: one bubble per umbrella company (rows grouped by Company in the hierarchy), sized by rolled-up hours under the same v2 harness (40% canvas budget, floor, whale ceiling). Identity hue lives at galaxy level; recency fades it; idle/ended galaxies fold into the Archive.
Click a multi-project galaxy → it expands in place into its solar systems (Project › Platform rows) as hue-inherited shades — the Identity path. Click a system inside → the view zooms: that galaxy becomes the canvas (breadcrumb top-left backs out). Click a system at full zoom → its planets (Platform · Navigator ID · login) fan out inside. A single-project galaxy skips all of this — click goes straight to the Time Tracker, like today.
Hues here follow the v2 palette; the colorblind-safe variant applies exactly as in v2.
